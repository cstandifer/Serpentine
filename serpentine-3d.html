How to play
  • Tilt your phone left or right to turn, the way the snake is facing. No phone? Arrow keys or a sideways drag.
  • Jump (for real, holding the phone) to clear low teal hurdles. Space or a tap works too.
  • Rose pillars are too tall to jump. Steer around them.
  • Walls can’t be jumped by any snake.
  • Gems make you longer. Don’t run into your tail, but you can jump it.
  • Eat the level’s gem goal to clear it and unlock the next level.
  • You get 3 lives per run. A crash restarts the level. Lose them all and the run is over.
