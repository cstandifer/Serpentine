How to play
  • Tilt your phone to steer: a little for a gentle curve, more for a tight one. No phone? Hold the arrow keys or drag sideways.
  • Jump (for real, holding the phone) to clear low lime hurdles. Space or a tap works too.
  • Pink pillars are too tall to jump. Steer around them.
  • Walls can’t be jumped by any snake.
  • Gems make you longer. Don’t run into your tail, but you can jump it.
  • Eat the level’s gem goal to clear it and unlock the next level.
  • You get 3 lives per run. A crash restarts the level. Lose them all and the run is over.
